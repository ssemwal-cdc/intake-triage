# Function list suggestions

Status: open. Source: June 30, 2026 org chart, supplied by the owner.

| Option | Gain | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| A. Finance sub-functions in full, other groups one entry each (recommended) | Main audience finds its team; triage groups cleanly | ~18 entries to keep in step with the chart |
| B. All ~60 sub-functions | Everyone finds their exact team | Goes stale fastest |
| C. Top-level groups only | Short, stable | Finance collapses to one entry |
| D. Free text only | Nothing to maintain | Same team arrives under many spellings |

Option A list: Finance, Accounting, Construction Accounting, Corporate Tax, Treasury, Investments, Supply Chain, Enterprise Risk Management & Site Compliance, Human Resources, Corporate Development, PMO, Delivery, Design & Solutions Engineering, Datacenter Operations, IT, Safety, ESG & Innovation, Customer & Marketing, Legal, Culture & Learning.

Open: mockup entries not on the chart: Capital Projects, Capital Markets, Opex Finance. Keep or drop.
